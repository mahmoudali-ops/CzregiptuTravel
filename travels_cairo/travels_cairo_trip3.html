Description for Cairo Small Group Tour

We pick you up early in the morning from your hotel by air-conditioned mini-bus.

During the journey to Cairo, you will enjoy the desert scenery and a short coffee break.

Upon arrival, we start with a guided visit to the Egyptian Museum, home to thousands of ancient treasures.

Afterwards, you will have lunch in a traditional restaurant and enjoy authentic Egyptian cuisine.

Next, we continue to Giza to see the world-famous pyramids, the Sphinx, and the Valley Temple.

Later, stroll through the vibrant Khan El Khalili Bazaar where you can shop for souvenirs and enjoy the local atmosphere.

In the afternoon, we drive back to Hurghada, arriving at your hotel in the evening.

Included

  • Pickup and return transfer from your hotel in Hurghada
  • Professional English-speaking guide
  • Entry to the Egyptian Museum
  • Lunch in a local restaurant
  • Visit to the Pyramids and Sphinx area in Giza
  • Transfers by air-conditioned vehicle

Not Included
